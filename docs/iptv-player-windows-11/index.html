Windows 11 setup

Install, load a source and start playback

  1. Open the official Microsoft Store listing on your Windows PC. Check its system requirements for your device and install the trial. No Android emulator is required.
  2. Choose the instructions for your source: M3U file or URL, Xtream Codes credentials, or Stalker Portal details.
  3. Enable Play in Internal VLC Player, or select an external player using Browse beside the player path.
  4. Open the Live tab, choose a category and double-click a channel. Movies and Series appear when your source supplies them.
  5. In supported internal Live TV playback, press F for fullscreen, ↑ or ↓ to change channels, and Esc to leave fullscreen.

Check your display and playback during the trial

Test window resizing and text readability at your normal Windows display scale. If controls appear too large or small, review Windows Settings → System → Display and use a scale appropriate for your screen. For the app's large-screen interface, follow the TV Mode user guide.

If a stream fails, test another item from the same source. If every item fails, confirm your source is active and the internal player is enabled or the external player path is correct. If the Store reports incompatibility, use its listed requirements; Windows 11 alone does not establish support for every device architecture.
