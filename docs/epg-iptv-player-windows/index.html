Troubleshooting

Why EPG may be missing or incorrect

If no guide appears, your source may not provide EPG or its guide data may not match the channel identifiers. If only some channels have listings, coverage may be incomplete. Program times also depend on the source data and device settings.

The 7-day trial lets you check the channels you actually use before deciding. Compare guide coverage, timing, logos and category matching on your own Windows PC.

FAQ

Windows IPTV EPG FAQ

Does BLAZIN provide EPG data?

No. BLAZIN displays compatible guide information supplied by your own legal source.

Can M3U and Xtream Codes both use EPG?

Yes, when the source provides compatible guide information.

Why do some channels have no program guide?

The source may have incomplete EPG coverage or channel-to-guide matching.

Can I test the EPG before buying?

Yes. Use the 7-day Microsoft Store trial with your own legal source.
